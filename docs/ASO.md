# ASO & Day-1 Launch — HoldMyWallet

## 1. Naming recommendation

**Do not ship the primary title as only “Wallet, Please Wait.”**

| Approach | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| Exact “Wallet, Please Wait” as title | Higher copycat / trademark / “spammy clone” risk on Apple & Google |
| **HoldMyWallet** as title + “Wallet Please Wait” in subtitle / keywords | Brandable, searchable, safer |

**Chosen primary brand:** HoldMyWallet  
**Tagline / keyword bridge:** Wallet, Please Wait — pause impulse buys

## 3. Store description (Play + App Store long form)

**Stop. Ask the internet. Then buy.**

HoldMyWallet (inspired by the viral “Wallet, Please Wait” idea) is the anti-impulse-buying social utility for online shoppers.

About to tap *Buy Now* on Shopee, Lazada, Amazon, or TikTok Shop? Submit the item, set a 1h / 6h / 24h timer, and let the Public Square—or your private circle—vote **Approve** or **Reject** with a real reason.

**Why it works**
- Mandatory one-sentence explanations (no empty troll taps)
- Live vote bars while the countdown runs
- Private Circle deep links for partner / group-chat veto power
- Savings tracker when you mark a rejected item as Skipped

**Built for Gen-Z & Millennials** who are into de-influencing, mindful spending, and roasting bad purchases—with kindness.

Download HoldMyWallet. Your wallet can wait.
